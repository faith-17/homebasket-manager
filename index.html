ESTIMATES FROM YOUR BOOKS

Profit & stock report

Estimate formulas: Estimated cost of goods sold = opening stock + stock purchases/restocks − closing stock − household goods taken − recorded stock losses. Estimated gross profit = sales − estimated cost of goods sold. Estimated net profit/loss = gross profit − household use − stock losses − operating expenses. Stock valuations and household/loss values are entered estimates, not product-level costing. Contributions, withdrawals, and transfers are shown separately and are not sales or expenses.
